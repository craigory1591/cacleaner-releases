Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information CACleaner and cacleaner.co.uk use. In short: CACleaner works on your PC, and we don't collect information about your files or what you clean. We keep only what's needed for your account.

What the app does on your PC

CACleaner scans and cleans files, browser data and settings on your computer. That all happens on your computer. We never receive the names, contents or details of your files, your browsing history or your cookies. Virus scans are done by Microsoft Defender on your PC.

Your account

When you create an account we keep your email address, the name you give, your password (stored only as a one-way scrambled hash, never as the password itself), when you registered, when your Pro ends, and the names of the PCs you've signed in on with when you last used them. We use this only to run your account and your Pro license.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Account checks: when you sign in, and when the app starts and every few hours, CACleaner checks your account with our server so your Pro works on every PC. It sends your sign-in token and your computer's name.
  • Update checks: CACleaner checks GitHub for new versions of itself. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • App and driver updates (Pro): the Software Updater and Driver Updater use Windows' own tools (winget and Windows Update), which contact Microsoft and app publishers.
  • Virus definitions: Microsoft Defender downloads its own updates from Microsoft.

The website

cacleaner.co.uk has no adverts and no tracking or analytics cookies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

How long we keep information

We keep your account for as long as you have it. If your account is deleted, its details are removed from our server.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email . You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Who we are

CACleaner is run by Craig Holmes, in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for this information.
