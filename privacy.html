Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information CACleaner and cacleaner.co.uk use. In short: CACleaner works on your PC, and we don't collect information about your files or what you clean.

What the app does on your PC

CACleaner scans and cleans files, browser data and settings on your computer. That all happens on your computer. We never receive the names, contents or details of your files, your browsing history or your cookies.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Update checks: CACleaner checks GitHub for new versions of itself. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • Pro license checks: if you activate Pro, CACleaner sends your license key and your computer's name to Lemon Squeezy to check the key is valid. This happens when the app starts and every few hours.
  • App and driver updates (Pro): the Software Updater and Driver Updater use Windows' own tools (winget and Windows Update), which contact Microsoft and app publishers.

When you buy Pro

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, billing address and payment details and shares your name, email and order details with us so we can support you. We never see your full card details. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy for how they handle your data.

The website

cacleaner.co.uk has no adverts and no tracking or analytics cookies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

How long we keep information

We keep order and support emails for as long as you're a customer and up to 6 years after, as UK tax rules require.

Who we are
